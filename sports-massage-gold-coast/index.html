Sports Massage FAQ

Sports Massage Questions, Gold Coast

How much does a sports massage cost? +

Sessions are $90 for 45 minutes, $120 for 60 minutes, $180 for 90 minutes, and $240 for 120 minutes. Appointments are centred on hands-on treatment with Parksy from start to finish.

Do I need to be an athlete to book a sports massage? +

No. Parksy works with people at different stages of training, from those who simply want to move and train well through to serious lifters, athletes, bodybuilders and competitive physique clients.

Does sports massage hurt? +

It can be firm, particularly through tight or overworked tissue, but pressure is matched to what your body presents with on the day rather than applied the same way to everyone.

How often should I get a sports massage? +

It depends on your training load and how well you recover. Many clients book every two to four weeks to stay ahead of restriction rather than waiting for pain to force an appointment.

Will I see the same therapist every time? +

Yes. Every appointment is with Parksy personally. There is no rotating roster and no re-explaining your history to someone new each visit.

Do you offer remedial massage as well as sports massage? +

Yes. Sports massage is the specialty, and remedial technique is part of how tightness, restriction and long-standing niggles are treated within your session.

What are Parksy's qualifications? +

Parksy is a qualified Personal Trainer and Remedial Massage Therapist with over 17 years in the health and fitness industry, working from Optimal Health Clinic in Mermaid Beach.

What is the difference between sports massage and relaxation massage? +

Relaxation massage is about unwinding. Sports massage is functional work aimed at muscle under genuine training load, clearing restriction so you move and perform better.
