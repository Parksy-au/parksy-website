Strength & Performance Coaching

Not every client wants bodybuilding-style programming. Some simply want a qualified Personal Trainer to build them a proper strength foundation, structure their gym training, and keep them accountable. Parksy offers exactly that for coaching clients through online coaching, whether you're brand new to the gym, returning to consistent training, or want general fitness and strength progression without the physique-competition focus.

Who This Is For

  • People new to structured training who want to start properly, with real coaching from day one
  • Clients returning to consistent training after a break
  • Anyone wanting general fitness and strength progression, without a bodybuilding or competition focus
  • Clients who want direct access to their coach, with video form check-ins and regular feedback and direct coaching from Parksy

As a qualified Personal Trainer with over 17 years in the health and fitness industry, Parksy builds programs around your training history and adjusts them as you progress, the same individualised approach used across all coaching services, just without the physique-competition emphasis.

Coaching FAQ

Personal Training & Coaching Questions

How is coaching delivered? +

Coaching is delivered online, with programming, check-ins and adjustments managed directly by Parksy.

What is the difference between physique coaching and personal training? +

Both are coached personally by Parksy, built for different starting points. Personal training suits those building a foundation or returning to consistent training. Physique and bodybuilding coaching is for clients pushing toward muscle, body composition and competition goals.

Do I need gym experience to start coaching? +

No. Programming is built around where you genuinely are, whether that is your first structured block of training or your tenth year under the bar.

What is included in the online coaching program? +

Individualised training programming, macro and nutrition structuring built around your training load, video form and movement check-ins, and direct coaching and regular feedback from Parksy.

Can beginners join, or is it only for competitive athletes? +

Yes. Parksy works with clients from first-timers through to competitive physique athletes, with programming built around the individual at every level.
