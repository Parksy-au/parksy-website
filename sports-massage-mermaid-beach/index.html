Sports massage questions

What is sports massage?+

Sports massage is a hands-on treatment approach that focuses on areas affected by training, movement, physical work or repetitive load. The session is shaped around what is presenting rather than following a fixed routine.

Do I need to be an athlete to book?+

No. Parksy works with athletes and people who train, but you do not need to compete or even train regularly. Sports and remedial massage can also suit people whose work or everyday physical demands are affecting how they feel and move.

What is the difference between sports massage and relaxation massage?+

Relaxation massage is primarily about switching off. Sports massage is more focused on the areas being affected by your movement, training or physical demands.

How long should I book?+

Choose the session length according to how much you want addressed. A 45 or 60 minute appointment can suit a more focused session, while 90 or 120 minutes allows more time when several areas need attention.

How much does a session cost?+

Sessions are $90 for 45 minutes, $120 for 60 minutes, $180 for 90 minutes and $240 for 120 minutes.

Will I see Parksy every time?+

Yes. Every sports and remedial massage appointment is with Parksy personally.
