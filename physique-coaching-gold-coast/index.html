Performance Coaching questions

Do I need to be an experienced lifter?+

No. Parksy coaches people at different stages, including those building their first structured approach to training. Coaching is adjusted to your current ability and goals.

Does Parksy coach athletes outside bodybuilding?+

Yes. Performance coaching can support athletes whose gym work needs to complement the demands of their sport, schedule and performance goals.

Does coaching include nutrition and macros?+

Yes, where appropriate. Calories, macronutrient targets and nutrition structure can be managed alongside training and adjusted as progress changes.

How often is my program adjusted?+

Programs are adjusted when training performance, progress or circumstances show that a change is needed, rather than simply because a fixed number of weeks has passed.

Can Parksy review my lifting technique?+

Yes. Video review and exercise feedback can be used where technique, setup or execution needs closer attention.

How do check-ins work?+

Check-ins review training, adherence, progress and any factors affecting the next coaching decision. Parksy then adjusts training or nutrition where needed.

What happens after I apply?+

Parksy reviews your training background, goals and what you are looking for from coaching, then confirms the next step if the service is appropriate for you.

Is coaching suitable if I train for a sport as well as the gym?+

Yes. Your gym programming can be organised around existing sport training so that strength and physique work supports, rather than competes with, your wider performance goals.

Coaching numbers are kept limited.

Parksy keeps his coaching roster at a size that allows him to remain directly involved in each client’s programming, reviews and training decisions.
