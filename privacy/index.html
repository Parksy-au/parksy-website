Privacy Policy

About This Policy

This policy explains how Ultimate Sports Massage by Parksy, operated by Michael Parks as a sole trader ("we", "us"), collects, uses, discloses and protects personal information, including health information, in connection with in-clinic sports and remedial massage in Mermaid Beach, online coaching and this website. We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number;
  • appointment and booking details;
  • health information relevant to your treatment, including health questionnaire responses, health conditions, injuries, concerns and treatment notes;
  • payment and transaction records for your appointments;
  • information in messages you send us, which may include health information you choose to share;
  • for coaching applications: your age, training experience, goals, whether the coaching fee suits your budget, why you are looking for coaching and when you would like to start;
  • for online coaching clients: training goals, progress photographs, body measurements, nutrition information, training and progress records, check-in information and training videos you send for technique feedback.

How We Collect and Use Information

We collect personal information directly from you, including when you complete the contact form or coaching application on this website, book an appointment or complete a health questionnaire through our online booking system, speak with us during a discovery call or appointment, or message us on Instagram. We collect health information only where it is reasonably necessary to provide safe and appropriate treatment or coaching, and with your consent.

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, manage appointments, bookings and payments, contact you about your appointments, provide massage treatment, assess coaching applications, provide online coaching, issue receipts you can use to claim with your private health fund, and meet our legal obligations. We do not claim from health funds on your behalf.

We do not currently send promotional marketing messages. If we do in future, we will comply with Australian consent and unsubscribe requirements.

Photographs, Testimonials and Clients Under 18

Progress photographs and testimonials are kept private and are only used publicly, including on this website or Instagram, with your separate permission. Online coaching is available to clients aged 14 and older. For clients under 18, consent from a parent or legal guardian is required before coaching begins and before any public use of photographs or testimonials.

Who We Share Information With

We use these service providers, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards contact form and coaching application submissions to us by email;
  • Google (Gmail), which hosts the email inbox where those submissions are received;
  • Acuity Scheduling (Squarespace), which provides our online booking and payment system and stores our massage client records; and
  • Instagram (Meta), if you contact us through Instagram.

Some of these providers are based overseas or store information outside Australia, so your information may be held or processed overseas.

Massage appointments take place at Optimal Health Clinic in Mermaid Beach. Optimal Health Clinic is a separate business and does not have access to our client records. Otherwise, we only use or disclose your personal information with your consent or where required or permitted by law.

Storage and Retention

Massage client records, including health questionnaires and treatment notes, are kept in Michael's own Acuity Scheduling account, and we do not keep separate paper treatment records. Coaching records are held privately by Michael. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference and loss, and from unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

We retain personal and health information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain appropriate business and treatment records, and meet applicable legal obligations. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or de-identify it.

Automated Decisions

Our online coaching application asks whether the $150 weekly coaching fee suits your budget. If you answer that it does not, the form automatically shows an alternative recommendation instead of the discovery-call booking step. This is not a formal rejection: your answers are still sent to Parksy, and you are welcome to contact us directly or apply again in the future.

Access, Correction and Complaints

You can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a complaint about how we have handled it, by emailing Michaelparks755@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available on this page. Our Terms & Conditions also apply to our services.
