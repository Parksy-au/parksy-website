Common Questions

Does performance coaching include nutrition and macros?+

Yes. Coaching can include calorie and macronutrient targets, nutrition structure and ongoing adjustments alongside training and progress review.

How much does a sports massage cost?+

Sessions are $90 for 45 minutes, $120 for 60 minutes, $180 for 90 minutes and $240 for 120 minutes.

Do I need to be an athlete to book a massage?+

No. Parksy works with active people and athletes, but you do not need to compete or train seriously to book. Treatment can also be useful when work, daily activity or general physical demands are affecting how you feel and move.

Will I see Parksy every time?+

Yes. Every sports massage appointment is with Parksy personally from start to finish.

Does Parksy coach beginners as well as experienced athletes?+

Yes. Coaching is built around the individual starting point. Parksy works with people developing their first structured approach to training through to experienced lifters, athletes, bodybuilders and competitive physique clients.

How is performance coaching delivered?+

Coaching is delivered online, with programming, check-ins, technique feedback and adjustments managed directly by Parksy.

What is the difference between performance coaching and personal training?+

Performance coaching manages the wider training process over time, including programming, progression, review and, where appropriate, nutrition. Personal training is typically centred on coached training sessions. Parksy’s performance coaching is built around the individual and their goals.

How do I get started?+

Sports massage can be booked directly online. Performance coaching begins with an application so Parksy can understand your training background, goals and what you are looking for from coaching.
